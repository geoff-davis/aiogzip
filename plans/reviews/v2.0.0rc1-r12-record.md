# R12 record: RC1 timing windows

Gate R12 of the [2.0.0rc1 plan](../RELEASE_2_0_0RC1_PLAN.md) runs the
timing checks deferred by R01, R03, R04, R08 and R15. Its acceptance
criterion is "no unexplained cost above 5%". The method, inputs and decision
rule are in the [pre-registration](v2.0.0rc1-r12-preregistration.md), which
Codex confirmed before anything was timed. The evidence is archived in
[`benchmarks/data/rc1/r12/`](../benchmarks/data/rc1/r12/README.md).

Status: awaiting Codex review and maintainer sign-off.

## Windows

Both windows passed the quiet gate on the 01:00 firing on 2026-10-09, so the
four later firings found nothing to do.

| Window | Started | Peak foreign CPU | `summarize.py window` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ab`: b2 `962bfe4` against `dd70af4` | 01:00 | 1.55 cores in one capture, 0.37 or less in every other | Accepted |

Each window took about 12 minutes, not the 40 to 50 the pre-registration

## Dispositions

### D1: `inspect()` and `verify()` on small files (R03)

**Measured.** In the `ab` window the small-file rows were slower than b2 in
every A/B pair on both engines, and flat in `aa`:

- stdlib: `inspect` +11.15% and `verify` +10.35% (incompressible);
- zlib-ng: `inspect` +13.30% and `verify` +12.69% (incompressible), +8.96%
  and +8.33% (compressible).

This is above the 10% line and attributable to the candidate, so it blocked
by default.

**Attribution.** The maintainer authorized a targeted test, and Codex
confirmed its pre-registration after three rounds
(`attrib-20261009-064918`, 144 valid processes, peak foreign CPU 0.35
cores). It timed `verify()` on 1 MiB files with parts of R03 reverted in the
candidate, over six rounds in a Williams-square order. On the incompressible
file:

| | stdlib | zlib-ng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total, b2 to candidate | +30.4 µs | +26.4 µs |
| Settled reads (6 per call) | +21.6 µs | +24.5 µs |
| Settled open | +0.5 µs | −0.9 µs |
| Settled close | +0.1 µs | +1.9 µs |
| All three reverted, against b2 | +0.4 µs | −4.5 µs |

The reads cost about 4 µs each, through `_initial_call` and `asyncio.shield`.
With all three reverted the candidate matches b2, so the cost lies in these
paths. The components do not sum exactly to the total (22 of 30 µs on stdlib),
so they are net costs of each patch, not an exact split.

**Repair.** At maintainer direction, PR #128 (`f7c03c0`, Codex approved)
moves the scanner's reads to the file reader's `_NativeSourceCall`:

- a successful read awaits the executor without a shield;
- a read still queued at cancellation is prevented and never touches the
  source.

The BC14 ledger row records the change.

**Re-check.** Codex confirmed this pre-registration after two rounds
(`recheck-20261009-073404`, 72 valid processes, peak foreign CPU 0.37
cores). It timed b2, `dd70af4` and `f7c03c0` in all six orders. The table
gives `verify()` by R12's statistic (minimum of medians, against b2):

| `verify()`, 1 MiB | `dd70af4` | `f7c03c0` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stdlib, incompressible | +8.11% | +4.46% |
| stdlib, compressible | +0.72% | +0.55% |
| zlib-ng, incompressible | +11.87% | +4.82% |
| zlib-ng, compressible | +10.48% | +6.52% |

**Disposition.** The maintainer accepted the remaining cost ("1",
2026-10-09) as R03's cancellation-safety cost: about 10 µs per call on
zlib-ng compressible (+6.52%), visible only on sub-millisecond calls.
Further savings from moving open and close to the cheaper path were
estimated at 2 to 5 µs and not pursued.

**Limit.** The re-check timed `verify()` only. `inspect()` runs the same
`_scan_gzip` reads, with member collection added, and was not separately
re-timed.

### N1: `Text read (bulk, identical fixture)`, zlib-ng, `ab` +22.49%

The +22.49% magnitude comes from one fast b2 capture: 4A at 0.788 ms,
while the other b2 captures were 0.959–0.981 ms. The candidate captures
(0.965–0.997 ms) overlap that range. Without 4A, the minimum-of-medians
delta is +0.70% (0.9652 against 0.9585 ms). The pair deltas were +1.5, +25.3
(the pair with 4A), −1.6 and +4.0. The evidence does not rule out a small
candidate cost on this row, but it puts any such cost well within 5%.

### N2: `CRC failure + salvage read() - 4MB`, stdlib

Within a single capture, this row's samples split between about 1.6 ms and
about 2.9 ms. Its minimum delta was −41.14% in `ab` and +51.61% in `aa`, so
it carries no signal. The b2 qualification record shows the same
single-capture swings on this row (for example +68.96% in G17's WP0 window).

## Conclusion

Two rows in the windows exceeded 5% without being attributable: N1 and N2,
both shown to be measurement artifacts. The one attributable cost above 5%
(D1) was investigated, partly repaired (#128) and accepted for the
remainder. With #128 merged, R12's criterion is met subject to Codex review
and maintainer sign-off.
