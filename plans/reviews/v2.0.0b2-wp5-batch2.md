# WP5 nightly batch 2: G06

Batch 2 runs the two remaining G06 series under the policy approved on
October 3 (`ee53619`): at most 2.5 foreign cores, with a running command
rejected only after three consecutive samples above that
([protocol](v2.0.0b2-wp5-timing-protocol.md)). The
[batch 1 record](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch1.md) gives the night-1 outcome that
prompted it. G06 stays open.

## Authorization

Batch 1's standing authorization ended once G06 zlib-ng started. On October 3
the maintainer explicitly authorized batch 2 as a new, bounded authorization:
these nights, these two series, the one permitted G06 zlib-ng follow-up and the
new policy.

- **Nights:** October 4 to 8, 2026 (five nights), 00:30–06:30 Pacific.
- **Series, in order:**

  | Series | Engine | A | B | Status |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `g06-stdlib` | stdlib | cea195d | 2f78925 | initial series; batch 1 deferred it before any block |
  | `g06-zlib-ng-followup` | zlib-ng | cea195d | 2f78925 | the one permitted follow-up series |

- The G06 zlib-ng initial series is batch 1's `g06-zlib-ng`: it started, and
  all four blocks were rejected for interference, so no row has a usable block.
  It is retained and is not repeated. The protocol allows one independent
  follow-up series for inconclusive rows, rerunning the whole matched size
  family; this follow-up reruns the full G06 matrix, which every row needs.
  Both series are reported separately.
- Retry, window and reserve rules are batch 1's: started series never repeat,
  series deferred before any block retry the next night, failures block. Each
  G06 series needs 3 hours 2 minutes left, so neither starts after 03:28.
