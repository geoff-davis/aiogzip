## Analysis rules

Each series uses the same four-block protocol as its initial series. Report the
initial and follow-up outcomes separately and explain any disagreement. A row
is resolved only when the follow-up has at least three usable blocks with a
consistent direction, and an effect larger than drift: same-sign effects
comparable to drift do not establish an improvement. Neither series is selected over the other, and a row is
not resolved because one series is faster. A row still inconclusive afterwards
needs an explicit maintainer disposition or leaves G08 open.
