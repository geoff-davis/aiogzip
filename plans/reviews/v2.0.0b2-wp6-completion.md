## Exit criteria (§10)

- **Binary no longer stores the Boolean pair.** `_read_broken` and
  `_read_validation_failed` are not slots and are never assigned in production
  code; an AST test checks every store or delete, including tuple targets.
- **Temporary text adapters are derived and read-only.** Both names are
  getter-only properties derived from `_read_health`; assigning either raises.
  Binary code never reads them; text's three reads are unchanged. The design
  record lists each temporary name with WP8 as its owner.
- **Transitions match C0.** `tests/test_read_health_transitions.py` (19 tests)
  follows the C0 transition table and asserts, for each case, health, EOF,
  exact retained salvage, position, live or discarded decoder, active call,
  closure, poison-observer events, later results and recovery.
  `tests/test_read_health.py` (13 tests) covers structure and post-transition
  invariants. Validation salvage stays recovery data followed by a terminal
  error, never clean EOF; an uncertain source read is never relabeled as
  salvage.
- **C0 semantic traces pass.** File-state, source-failure, opening and
  opening-cleanup trace tests pass on both engines at the branch head.
- **Full suite passes.** Both engine suites: 3,681 passed, 1 skipped.
  All-file prek passes.
- **Affected benchmarks stay within policy** (below).

## Accepted cleanup hardening

Two narrow C0 cleanup gaps were fixed, each accepted by the maintainer and
changing no public result or C0 trace ([design](../design/v2.0.0b2-wp6-read-health.md#accepted-cleanup-hardening)):
a raising poison observer can no longer skip `decoder.discard()`, and an
exceptional exit that aborts a reader with native work in flight no longer
leaves the closed reader's decoder live.

## Benchmarks

Method A, chosen by the maintainer: `io,micro,read_health` against clean C0
and candidate `d1f13d4` worktrees, the same frozen venv, both engines, eight
captures per engine in ABBA ABBA order with five repeats each, pinned to CPU 13,
compared by per-benchmark minimum of capture medians. The window ran once at
02:15:30 PDT on October 5 after its quiet gate passed; all 16 captures were
valid and clean (peak foreign CPU 0.31 cores). Codex reviewed the scripts,
units and summarizer before registration.

- **zlib-ng:** no benchmark more than 5% slower; the largest increase is
  +1.49% (binary readline, 17-byte chunks).
- **stdlib:** every benchmark within ±1.8% except two flagged ones, followed
  up with a targeted micro-test as CLAUDE.md requires:

| Flagged benchmark (stdlib) | Window minimum | Window pairs | Micro-test, best | Micro-test, median of medians |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| CRC failure + salvage `read()`, 4 MB | +18.92% | −0.0, +0.5, +19.6, +18.5 | −0.68% | −0.45% |
| Text large reads (`read(n)`, 1 MiB) | +5.66% | +9.6, +0.6, −0.1, +5.7 | +0.05% | −0.00% |

The micro-test timed each workload per operation in fresh processes on stdlib:
ten rounds, C0 then candidate in every round (not ABBA), with warmup and 300
(text) or 150 (salvage) timed iterations, pinned to CPU 13. These execution
facts are implementer-reported; the archived driver is Claude's transcription
of the command it ran. The text probe times the benchmark's scored quantity
(the sized `read(n)`). After the first, cold text pair, the per-round median
deltas are −0.40% to +0.30% for text and −1.03% to +0.85% for salvage.

The isolated timing does not reproduce the window's pattern: the salvage
result split across the run rather than holding in every pair, and the same
benchmark on zlib-ng was +0.28%. Neither flag shows a stable candidate cost
above the 5% policy. This is not a zero-overhead claim: WP6 does change the
binary guards, and text's legacy reads become derived properties, so a small
cost below the measurement's resolution is possible.

**Maintainer disposition (2026-10-05):** accepted. The two flags are not
reproduced above policy by targeted isolated timing; the affected benchmarks
are within policy.

The new `read_health` category confirms the seek, recovery and salvage paths
directly: on both engines every case is within ±1% apart from the salvage
flag above.

Evidence, including the raw captures, monitor log, scripts, units and
micro-test, is archived in
[`data/b2/wp6-g10`](../benchmarks/data/b2/wp6-g10/README.md).
