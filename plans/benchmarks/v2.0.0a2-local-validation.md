## Correctness and Coverage

The zlib-ng-active branch-coverage run completed with 1,657 passed and one
skipped at 92.81% coverage. The forced-stdlib run, with zlib-ng still
installed and the engine-reload module isolated, completed with 1,624 passed,
one skipped, and 92.91% coverage. All 33 isolated engine-reload tests passed.

header boundary, bounded highly-compressible streaming allocations, and the
decoder scaling regression tests.

build passed.

## Clean-Wheel Smoke

The wheel was force-installed with its `fast` extra into fresh uv environments
using CPython 3.11.11 and CPython 3.14.5. On both interpreters,
`scripts/smoke_wheel.py` passed with zlib-ng active and with
`AIOGZIP_ENGINE=stdlib`.

The smoke covers:

- direct `GzipEncoder` and `GzipDecoder`;
- `compress_chunks()` and `decompress_chunks()`;
- whole-file `write()` and `read()`;
- `inspect()` and `verify()`;
- `python -m aiogzip verify`;
- CLI version and package version consistency; and
- active engine selection.

Final artifacts must be rebuilt and these checks repeated after the Framework
benchmark record, final version, and dated changelog are committed.
