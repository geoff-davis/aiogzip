### Diagnostic changes

Exception types and inheritance are unchanged. [Stability](../../docs/stability.md)
freezes no complete message except the `max_decompressed_size` prefix. The
candidate adds or newly reaches these:

- BC11: the terminal broken-stream `OSError` after a text seek that failed
  or was cancelled after moving the binary reader.
- BC13: a native close failure during a cancelled close becomes the
  cancellation's `__cause__`; a text observer failure during the abort latch
  is a note on the cancellation.
- BC14: the scan's cleanup precedence. An outside cancellation or
  interrupt during cleanup outranks an ordinary (`Exception`) scan failure
  and carries it as `__context__`. An original cancellation or interrupt
  keeps precedence, with a cleanup failure noted; after the R13 repair a
  repeated cancellation is not itself noted, only a close failure that
  settlement chained to it. A close failure after an ordinary scan failure
  is a note on the scan error.
- BC15: the note `Context-exit cleanup also failed: <error>!r`; the CLI
  reports any other `Exception` as `<Type>: <message>`, with exit status 2.

## Remaining limitations

The b2 limitations stand, apart from the ones RC1 fixed:

- **Small-read amplification (F6, G07).** Unchanged from b2.
- **Text-cookie recovery (BC10).** Unchanged; `seek(0)` is the guaranteed
  recovery, now also after BC11.
- **Line size (G06)** and **cooperative fairness (G08).** Unchanged.
- **Per-call costs (G17 D1–D4)** against b1, unchanged. R12 found no new
  cost above 5% against b2 except the accepted `verify()` row; `inspect()`
  was not re-timed after #128.
- **Cancellation can wait for blocked native I/O** (BC1/BC2, now also BC13
  and BC14), by design. The b2 caveat that a cancelled `close()` could
  return before the native close finished (G19 F2) is fixed by BC13.
- **Concurrent `tell()` during `readlines()` (G19 F3).** Deferred, as
  before.
- **`writelines()` with empty `str` subclasses.** Only exact empty `str`
  values are dropped from the pending batch; empty subclass instances are
  stored as in b2 (R13 Codex finding 4, Claude N2).
- **Shielded-future logging on Python 3.14+ (R13 Claude F2).** A native
  close that fails after a cancellation also reports "exception in shielded
  future" to the loop's exception handler, although the exception is
  retrieved and chained correctly. The mechanism is `_settle_before_cancel`,
  unchanged since b2; deferred at the maintainer's direction (2026-10-09).
- **Opening cleanup notes.** A repeated cancellation during `_acquire_path`'s
  close of a late open is noted as `Opening cleanup also failed:
  CancelledError(...)`, as b2's tests pin; if that close also fails, its
  error is lost. Deferred (R13 Claude F1, related b2 defect).

## Review questions

Each reviewer answers every question against the exact SHA, with evidence.

1. **Native completion before cleanup.** Can a cancelled `close()`, context
   exit, abort, `inspect()` or `verify()` run cleanup, or return, before
   native work on the same file has finished? Is every owned close, once
   submitted, actually run?
2. **Late I/O results.** Is every native open, read or close that
   completes after its caller stopped waiting either applied exactly once
   or settled into a terminal state? In particular, can the scan's
   `_NativeSourceCall` reads (`track_position=False`) drop or double-apply a
   read?
3. **Resource ownership and the close latch.** Does every acquired
   resource have exactly one owner on every path, including the scan's late
   path acquisition? Does BC13's abort latch mark CLOSED only when the
   native close really returned, and leave the handle OPEN for a retry
   otherwise? Are borrowed files left open?
4. **Text seek transaction (BC11).** Does every failed or cancelled text
   `seek()` either change nothing or leave the reader BROKEN until a
   completed `seek(0)`? Can the snapshot share mutable state with the live
   reader, and can a no-effect failure stay retryable when it should not, or
   the reverse?
5. **End-relative seek (BC12).** Is buffered output counted exactly once in
   every state? Is EOF still set only after trailer validation, and is
   `max_decompressed_size` still enforced on the seek's own drain?
6. **`writelines()` bound (R08; no observable change, so no ledger row).** Is the pending batch
   bounded for every input mix, with the bytes written unchanged, including
   the byte-order mark of an empty UTF-16 or UTF-32 write?
7. **Cancellation accounting and error reporting (BC15).** Is the abort's
   cancellation request consumed exactly once however the call ends, while
   outside requests stay counted? Are exception types and precedence
   unchanged, with cleanup failures only added as notes? Is the CLI's exit
   status and `--json` output right?
8. **Differential predicates.** Are `BC11-TEXT-SEEK-FAILURE`,
   `BC12-SEEK-END` (and its `fixed_root` run) and BC2 clause S exact, so
   that none claims a difference outside its ledger row?
9. **Public contract.** Is the candidate within the stability policy for a
   release candidate: no signature, exception-type, dataclass or manifest
   change, and only compatible fixes?
10. **Performance.** Is the R12 record honest, and is any cost above 5%
    against b2 unexplained?
11. **Test-only changes.** Do the #131 flake repairs and the #132 fixture
    level keep their tests sensitive to the defects they guard, without
    weakening any gate?
12. **Release inputs.** Do the changelog and ledger match the code? Does
    the reviewed SHA ship the recorded artifacts, and does the publish
    workflow upload exactly the files it validated?
