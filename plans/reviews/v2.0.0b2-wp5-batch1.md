# WP5 nightly batch 1: remaining timing series

On October 2 the maintainer approved running the remaining WP5 timing series as
one sequential nightly batch, under a standing authorization for a bounded set
of nights, with one review of the plan and units instead of one per window.
They also approved gating windows on foreign CPU only
([protocol](v2.0.0b2-wp5-timing-protocol.md)). G06, G07 and G08 stay open; no
gate closes unattended.

## Authorization

- **Nights:** October 3 to 9, 2026, inclusive (seven nights). The timer fires
  at 00:30:00 Pacific on each; the driver refuses outside 00:30–06:30 Pacific
  and outside those dates.
- **Series, in order:**

  | Series | Gate | Engine | A | B |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `g08-throughput-zlib-ng-wp4` | G08 throughput | zlib-ng | aceca4b | 2f78925 |
  | `g08-throughput-zlib-ng-wp5` | G08 throughput | zlib-ng | f73ae8d | 2f78925 |
  | `g08-scheduling-stdlib-wp4` | G08 scheduling | stdlib | aceca4b | 2f78925 |
  | `g08-scheduling-zlib-ng-wp4` | G08 scheduling | zlib-ng | aceca4b | 2f78925 |
  | `g07-both` | G07 | both, alternating | — | 2f78925 |
  | `g06-stdlib` | G06 | stdlib | cea195d | 2f78925 |
  | `g06-zlib-ng` | G06 | zlib-ng | cea195d | 2f78925 |

- **Retries:** a series that has started any measurement block is never run
  again, whatever its outcome; a later continuation or follow-up needs its own
  review. A series that deferred with every block unstarted is retried on the
  next authorized night. A failed, running, unreadable or foreign attempt blocks
  that series until someone investigates.
- **Windows:** each series gets a fresh window from launch to the earlier of
  the night's end and four hours. It starts only if that leaves its measurement
  reserve plus the 120-second quiet period: 32 minutes for G07 and G08, and
  3 hours 2 minutes for G06. A series that doesn't fit waits for the next night.
  Series run one at a time in one process holding a batch-wide lock.

## Driver and pins

The [driver](../../scripts/run_b2_nightly_batch.py) was added in `558687b`;
Codex reviewed four revisions to no findings. It runs from the harness
checkout, now pinned at `558687b`. Against the previous harness `e6d8422`, the
measurement scripts, benchmarks, `uv.lock` and `pyproject.toml` are unchanged;
the runner differs only in the foreign-CPU-only quiet policy (`ebcc67d`).

The plan pins every checkout commit, the interpreter and lockfile hashes and
the quiet policy, plus each series' manifest template hash: the SHA-256 of the
whole manifest apart from window and output. Each night, every prepared
manifest must match both, or the night stops before anything is written or
launched; earlier attempts must match them too. The unit passes the SHA-256 of
the exact plan bytes, so nights, window, series and order can't change after
review.

Each series' template equals its reviewed draft against 2f78925
([G06/G07](v2.0.0b2-wp5-g06-g07-2f78925-drafts.md),
[G08](v2.0.0b2-wp5-g08-2f78925-drafts.md)) apart from the harness pin

The service uses `KillMode=control-group`, a 15-second stop timeout and a
6 h 1 min runtime cap. The timer is `Persistent=false` with one-second accuracy
and no randomized delay; `systemd-analyze calendar` gives exactly the seven
nights. The plan loads only with its hash, `--templates` reproduces every
template hash from the pinned checkouts, `systemd-analyze --user verify`
passes, and no batch output exists.

## Review

Collection and review happen together once the batch ends or every series has
started: one collector bundle per started attempt, deferrals preserved, and one
Codex review of all outcomes. Each series then needs its own gate disposition.

Codex reviewed the plan and units before registration and found no issues: the
hashes, pins, reproduced templates, draft equivalence, calendar, runtime cap and
record wording all check out. Both units are linked to the reviewed files and
byte-identical to them. The timer is enabled and waiting for 00:30 PDT on
October 3; the service is inactive with `MainPID=0`, and lingering is enabled.

## Night 1 outcome and retirement

The batch ran once, from 00:30 to 02:14 PDT on October 3, launching all seven
series:

| Series | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| `g08-throughput-zlib-ng-wp4` | complete, 4 of 4 blocks |
| `g08-throughput-zlib-ng-wp5` | complete, 4 of 4 blocks |
| `g08-scheduling-stdlib-wp4` | incomplete: block 3 rejected for interference |
| `g08-scheduling-zlib-ng-wp4` | complete, 4 of 4 blocks |
| `g07-both` | incomplete: round 6 rejected for interference |
| `g06-stdlib` | deferred: interference during warmup A, no block started |
| `g06-zlib-ng` | incomplete: all four blocks rejected for interference |

Each rejection was triggered by a single 5-second sample above the 1.0-core
ceiling: G08 scheduling stdlib at 01:03:16 (2.82 cores), G07 at 01:14:45 (1.02),
G06 stdlib's warmup at 01:21:35 (1.07), and G06 zlib-ng at 01:45:02 (1.21),
02:00:02 (1.20), 02:03:17 (1.44) and 02:14:18 (1.02). Other above-ceiling
samples, at 01:00:05, 01:15:05–10, 02:00:07 and 02:03:22, fell in a quiet
period before a series or after a block's triggering sample. Most coincide with the quarter-hour
snapshot and hourly backup jobs; that is timing correlation, not attribution.
The collector reports no errors for any attempt.

On October 3 the maintainer approved tolerating brief spikes and raising the
ceiling (`ee53619`, [protocol](v2.0.0b2-wp5-timing-protocol.md)). Because the
G06 blocks cross every quarter-hour, the batch 1 timer was disabled and its
units unlinked before night 2, so that `g06-stdlib` would not start an initial
series under the old policy. The remaining G06 work continues in
[batch 2](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch2.md). Results review for the started series
follows separately.
