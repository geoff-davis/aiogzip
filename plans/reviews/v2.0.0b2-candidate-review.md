### Diagnostic changes

Exception types and inheritance are unchanged; [stability](../../docs/stability.md)
freezes no complete message except the `max_decompressed_size` prefix. The
candidate adds or newly reaches these messages:

- BC3 opening ownership: `ConcurrentOperationError` with `"open() is
  already in progress"` and `"close() called while open() is in progress"`
  (binary and text), and `"context exit called while open() is in
  progress"` (binary; a clean text context exit during opening surfaces
  the `close()` message). When
  cleanup after a failed or cancelled open also fails, the raised exception
  carries the note `Opening cleanup also failed: …`.
- `RuntimeError` `"aiofiles source belongs to a different event loop"` and
  the `sink` equivalent, raised when a native file is used from a loop other
  than the one that owns it.
- `OSError` `"write aborted because the gzip file was closed while the call
  was active"` and the `flush` equivalent (BC8). The `read` form existed in
  b1 and now also covers native rewinds (BC9).
- The terminal broken-stream `OSError` now covers a cancelled or failed
  custom source read (BC2), a failed text salvage (BC7), text
  `writelines([])` on a broken writer (BC8) and the failed-abort case (WP8).
- Two private `RuntimeError` assertions, `native source work has not
  settled` and `gzip binary file already has text observers attached`,
  guard internal invariants and are not reachable through the public API.

## Remaining limitations

- **Small-read amplification (F6, G07).** `read(1)` can inflate and retain
  much more plaintext than it returns. Eager drain is kept for b2;
  [errors](../../docs/errors.md) gives the memory guidance.
- **Text-cookie recovery (BC10).** After an integrity failure,
  `seek(saved_cookie)` may be refused; `seek(0)` is the guaranteed recovery.
- **Line size (G06).** A returned line can be arbitrarily large; no
  constant-memory `readline()` is claimed and no line-size limit was added
  ([WP5](v2.0.0b2-wp5-completion.md)).
- **Fairness is cooperative (G08).** Checkpoints bound the work between
  yields, not wall-clock time; a single large synchronous step still holds
  the loop ([streaming](../../docs/streaming.md)).
- **Per-call costs (G17 D1–D4).** About 6–8% on `flush()` and 6–9% on
  `read1()`, `readinto1()` and small-buffer `readline()` against b1.
- **Cancellation can wait for blocked native I/O** (BC1/BC2), by design.
- **Deferred (plan §18):** AnyIO/Trio, indexed/zran access, new engines,
  raw DEFLATE, default buffered writes, free-threaded claims, codec-only
  packaging, and the §18 opportunities (demand-bounded reader, broader
  resource budgets, validated-member events, execution policy, draining
  helpers, striped JSONL). Python 3.15 qualification is informational only
  and retained for later. The parked RC hardening program is outside b2.

## Review questions

Each reviewer answers every one against the exact SHA, with evidence. The
first four are §16's emphasis.

1. **Actual native completion.** Can cancellation, shutdown or context
   exit let cleanup run before native work on the same handle has finished?
2. **Ownership of late I/O results.** Is every native read, write, flush or
   seek that completes after its caller stopped waiting either applied
   exactly once or settled into a terminal state, never dropped or applied
   twice?
3. **Opening and closing resource publication.** Does every acquired
   resource have exactly one owner on every path, with no late resurrection
   and with external objects left caller-owned on failure?
4. **Masked input loss.** Can a later gzip member, or a retry on a custom
   source, hide input consumed but not delivered by an earlier failed or
   cancelled read?
5. **Cookie normalization.** Do text cookies replay every unpublished
   character, and is BC10's refusal confined to recovery after failure?
6. **Exception allowlisting.** Are the differential predicates
   (`BC7-TEXT-SALVAGE`, `BC8-WRITER-ABORT`, `BC9-REWIND-ABORT`,
   `BC10-TEXT-COOKIE-RECOVERY`, the BC2/BC3 named corrections) exact, so
   that none accepts a difference outside its ledger row?
7. **Cumulative performance.** Are the G17 dispositions honest, and is any
   cost above 5% unexplained?
8. **Health authority.** Is there exactly one read-health authority, with
   text never deciding health on its own?
9. **Rollback aliasing.** Can a rollback checkpoint share mutable state with
   the live text origin or pending batch?
10. **Temporary seam deletion.** Is the WP6 compatibility seam fully gone
    (§12.3), with nothing reading the old fields?
11. **Release inputs.** Do the changelog and draft release notes match the
    code and ledger, and does the reviewed SHA ship the G20 artifacts?

1. **Actual native completion: yes.** `_settle_before_cancel()` shields and
   retrieves executor results before propagating cancellation;
   `_NativeSourceCall` separately records worker entry and terminal
   publication. File reads, seeks, writes, flushes, acquisition and context
   abort use those settlement boundaries before cleanup. Evidence:
   `_codec_async.py:52`, `_source_io.py:43`, `_binary.py:1846`, `:1922`,
   `:2145`; `test_native_work_settlement.py`, `test_source_read_settlement.py`,
   `test_write_abort_settlement.py`, `test_open_qualification.py`. Concern:
   cancellation may wait indefinitely for genuinely blocked native I/O, the
   documented BC1/BC2 policy.
2. **Ownership of late I/O results: yes.** A queued native call is
   atomically prevented or allowed to start. A started read result is
   retrieved once and retained in `_pending_compressed_chunk`; a cancelled
   seek poisons the old decoder state; started sink calls settle before
   cancellation or close. The `2f78925` first compression result is consumed
   exactly once or recognized as complete before the driver is entered.
   Evidence: `_source_io.py:92`, `_binary.py:1852`, `_streaming.py:177`,
   `_codec_async.py:104`, `tests/test_stream_first_step.py`. Concern: none.
3. **Opening and closing resource publication: yes.** Acquisition and
   initialization stay local until the unsuspended publication at
   `_file = resource`. A failed path acquisition closes the locally owned
   resource; external objects stay unpublished and caller-owned. Cleanup
   failure precedence is explicit. Evidence: `_binary.py:384`,
   `_opening.py:29`, `_binary.py:1948`; `test_b2_open_ownership.py`,
   `test_open_settlement.py`, `test_open_qualification.py`. Concern: none.
4. **Masked input loss: no path found.** Custom calls compare synchronous
   source positions and poison the reader when consumption is uncertain.
   Cancelled native reads either never enter or retain the exact completed
   result. Retry is allowed only after a proven no-effect failure or a
   completed recovery. Evidence: `_binary.py:1813`, `:1846`;
   `test_source_read_settlement.py`; the BC2 lossy and replay model in
   `tests/stateful/differential.py:1021`. Concern: custom sources without a
   synchronous checkpoint become terminal after an uncertain failure, the
   documented compatibility correction.
5. **Cookie normalization: yes.** `tell()` uses a separately published
   pending origin during unpublished reads. Rollback keeps the original
   buffer origin and every newly decoded character. Cookie restoration
   replays the recorded decoder and newline state. BC10 is confined to an
   unhealthy reader whose cookie needs a nonzero binary seek; a direct
   `seek(0)` remains recovery. Evidence: `_text.py:509`, `:557`, `:1172`;
   `test_text_origin_transitions.py`, `test_wp5_longline_preflight.py`,
   `test_text_cookie_recovery.py:53`. Concern: the BC10 refusal is current
   behavior, not a permanent API guarantee; the ledger and test name say so.
6. **Exception allowlisting: yes, fail-closed.** Predicates are selected per
   reference, claim individual event or final-field differences, reject
   double claims, and leave any unclaimed difference as a failure. BC2 also
   requires the lossy model's whole trace to match b1 and rejects any model
   violation, including one at an otherwise matching event. BC10 is b1-only
   and requires the cookie seek to be the sole difference. Evidence:
   `tests/stateful/differential.py:103`, `:297`, `:986`, and the positive
   and near-miss matrix in `tests/stateful/test_differential.py`. Concern:
   none; no blanket exception normalization.
7. **Cumulative performance: honest; no established cost above 5% is
   unexplained.** D1–D4 attribute and accept measured 5–9% costs from
   settlement and ownership changes. D5 is demonstrated A/A bimodality.
   D6–D8 are initial flags that balanced reruns did not reproduce as stable
   candidate costs, and the record claims no cause. D9 meets its registered
   criterion. Evidence: the [G17](v2.0.0b2-wp10-qualification.md#g17-performance)
   windows, investigations and dispositions. Concern: the D1–D4 per-call
   costs are real and are disclosed in the changelog and release notes.
8. **Health authority: yes.** Binary owns one `_read_health` slot. Text's

9. **Rollback aliasing: none found.** The live origin is one mutable
