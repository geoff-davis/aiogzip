# Follow-up quiet-policy calibration: predeclared method

The previous single-operation calibration failed its 3% spread criterion. Preserve
that result. The maintainer authorized a follow-up with controlled warmup and
longer observations; no quiet-runner policy has been relaxed for qualification.

The stream measurement harness now has optional throughput-only, warmup-duration
and batch-duration arguments. Defaults retain single-operation measurements.
For this experiment:

- Use identical committed source in every capture, the same committed harness,
  copied environment and CPU 0 pin, with both engines and all 32 throughput cases.
- Require 120 quiet seconds with foreign CPU at most one core and load1/load5 at
  most 0.5 before starting. Continue monitoring foreign CPU during measurements.
- Allow up to 15 minutes for the start condition, then a separate 20 minutes for
  measurement. Timeout is distinct from detected interference.
- Discard one complete warmup capture per engine before comparison captures.
- Before each case, discard at least 0.25 seconds of recorded timed operation work.
- Each of seven retained samples sums at least 0.1 seconds of original timed
  intervals from at least three complete operations. This also lengthens the
  observations for cases already slower than 100 ms; warmup uses at least three
  operations too. Record raw operation durations and counts,
  and report their arithmetic mean per operation. Record total batch wall time
  separately to expose untimed validation/setup costs and the actual duty cycle.
- Run engine order stdlib, zlib-ng, stdlib, zlib-ng, zlib-ng, stdlib, zlib-ng,
  stdlib for four comparison captures per engine, following the two warmup captures.
- For every case, require max/min of the four capture minima to differ by at most
  3%. No exclusions based on observed results. Failure does not authorize repeated
  runs until a passing subset appears.

Complete-output validation occurs after each operation outside its timed interval,
including inside batches. This keeps the original timing boundary but validation
and setup between operations affect cache/thermal state. These are batch-mean
throughput observations, not single-operation latency. Do not compare their minima
directly with the earlier unbatched minima or treat this as G07 latency evidence.
Warmup cannot prove fixed frequency/temperature; retain raw results and limitations.

Commit and review the sampler before collecting evidence from a clean checkout.
Keep the failed first experiment and these new results separate. No overnight
reschedule or G06/G07/G08/G17 gate closure follows automatically.
