# S06 record: RC2 timing windows

Gate S06 of the [2.0.0rc2 plan](../RELEASE_2_0_0RC2_PLAN.md) times S02's
change on the offload path, and any other hot-path change, against rc1. Its
acceptance criterion is "no unexplained cost above 5%". The method, inputs and
decision rule are in the [pre-registration](v2.0.0rc2-s06-preregistration.md),
which Codex confirmed before anything was timed. The evidence is archived in
[`benchmarks/data/rc2/s06/`](../benchmarks/data/rc2/s06/README.md).

Status: Codex approved the record (`dbb29af`). Awaiting the maintainer's
sign-off.

## Windows

Both windows passed the quiet gate on the 01:00 firing on 2026-10-10, so the
four later firings found nothing to do.

| Window | Started | Peak foreign CPU | `summarize.py window` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ab`: rc1 `d382f17` against `0b9c7c4` | 01:00 (gate passed 01:02) | 0.36 cores | Accepted |
| `aa`: `0b9c7c4` against itself | 01:12 (gate passed 01:14) | 1.23 cores in one capture, 0.33 or less in every other | Accepted |

Each window's captures took about 10 minutes. The capture count, steps and
row inventory are as registered, and `summarize.py` checked every row against
`runner-rows.json`. The units were unlinked after the run.

## Results against rc1

One row exceeds 5% by minimum: `CRC failure + salvage read() - 4MB` on
stdlib (+59.59%, N1 below). Every other row is within 5% on both engines,
except two zlib-ng rows that are faster by more than 5%: `header filename
65536 bytes one-feed` (−6.60%) and `open/close x200` (−5.72%). The rows that measure S02 are:

| Path | Row | stdlib | zlib-ng |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Path open and owned close | `open/close x200` | −4.81% | −5.72% |
| Path open and close, inspection | `inspect`/`verify`/`_read_discard`, all fixtures | within −1.5% to +0.7% | within −2.9% to +1.6% |
| Offloaded compression | 262,144-byte `write`, `writelines` and `batch-262144` rows | −1.03% to −0.32% | −0.12% to +0.03% |
| Offloaded compression | `io` `Binary I/O (bulk, single write/read)`, `Text write (bulk)` | +0.02% to +0.12% | +0.02% to +0.31% |
| Offloaded compression | `concurrency`, `memory` | −0.47% to −0.23% | −0.61% to +0.42% |

`open/close x200` is faster on all eight adjacent pairs, both engines
(pairs −3.6% to −6.3%), while the `aa` control puts the same row within
+0.8%. This fits the change: an uncancelled settlement now creates one waiter
future and no shield, and each open settles its path acquisition and the
reader's native seekability probe, and each close settles one native close. R15
recorded this row at +3.91% (stdlib) and +4.59% (zlib-ng) against b2, so
much of that cost has been recovered.

The registration does not claim decoder offload as covered (Codex's
correction), and these results do not change that. The 64 KiB-record writes,
the `batch-65536` rows and the 64 KB-chunk binary I/O row compress inline,
below the 256 KiB offload threshold, so they are not counted as S02
coverage.

S01 and S03 change only abort and cancelled-open paths, which no row reaches.

## Dispositions

### N1: `CRC failure + salvage read() - 4MB`, stdlib, `ab` +59.59%

This is the same bimodal row as R12's N2. Within single captures its samples
split between about 1.6 ms and about 3.0 ms, on both sides:

| Capture | Median (ms) | Samples (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1A | 3.00 | 1.60 1.67 3.00 3.03 3.04 |
| 2B | 2.98 | 1.60 1.66 2.98 2.98 3.02 |
| 3B | 2.86 | 1.64 1.65 2.86 2.97 2.99 |
| 4A | 1.73 | 1.60 1.66 1.73 2.97 2.98 |
| 5A | 2.87 | 1.63 1.71 2.87 2.94 2.96 |
| 6B | 2.97 | 2.96 2.96 2.97 2.98 2.98 |
| 7B | 2.76 | 1.58 1.65 2.76 2.98 3.03 |
| 8A | 2.88 | 1.69 1.76 2.88 2.98 3.01 |

The minimum of medians is decided by whether any capture's median lands in
the fast mode. Here only 4A's did, so the delta is +59.59%. Without 4A, the
delta is −3.77% (2.762 against 2.870 ms). The pair deltas were −0.8, +65.5
(the pair with 4A), +3.6 and −3.9.

The `aa` control, where both sides are the same commit, shows the same flip
the other way: −41.32%, from its capture 7B at 1.69 ms. R12 recorded −41.14%
(`ab`) and +51.61% (`aa`) on this row. Both commits show both modes: 9 of rc1's 20
samples and 6 of the candidate's 20 are in the fast mode. The row is a salvage read of a 4 MB file;
S02 can change only its open (acquisition and seekability probe) and its
close. On zlib-ng the
row is −2.40% (`ab`) and +0.90% (`aa`). The flag is a measurement artifact,
not a candidate cost.

### Other rows above 5% in a single pair

None of these is more than 5% slower by minimum, so no disposition is
required. They are listed for completeness:

- `batch-262144 10B records`, stdlib: +3.51% by minimum, with pairs +23.9
  and +24.0. The `aa` control flags the same row at +16.86%, with one pair at
  +199.1%: this row is noisy on stdlib.
- `header filename 65536 bytes one-feed`, zlib-ng: −6.60% by minimum, pairs
  +9.8, −12.5, +9.7 and +1.3. A 34 µs row; the `aa` control's pairs reach
  ±6.5%.
- In the `aa` control on zlib-ng, `Text read (bulk, identical fixture)` is
  −18.56%, from one pair. It is the same row and the same pattern as R12's N1,
  on one commit.

## Conclusion

The one row above 5% (N1) is a known bimodal measurement artifact that flips
in the `aa` control too. No unexplained slowdown above 5% was established;
the windows do not exclude a small candidate cost below that threshold. The
measured effect of S02 is an improvement: `open/close x200` is about 5%
faster on both engines. S06's criterion is met, pending the maintainer's
sign-off.
