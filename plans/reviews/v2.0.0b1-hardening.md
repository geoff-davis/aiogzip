The complete suite covers property-based valid streams; malformed headers,
bodies, trailers, CRC, ISIZE, FHCRC, reserved flags, padding, and trailing
data; concatenated members and live/completed metadata; abandonment with GC
disabled; binary/text overlap; cancellation and poisoning; recovery-data
sequencing; seek and rewind; text cookies and newline modes; partial sink
writes and error attribution; strict-size and decompression limits; runtime
API manifests; and typing fixtures. No correctness failure was waived.

The exact-minimum details and artifact provenance are recorded separately in
`plans/dependencies/v2.0.0b1-minimum-dependencies.md` and
`plans/reviews/v2.0.0b1-installed-artifacts.md`.

## Performance and resource evidence

`plans/benchmarks/v2.0.0b1-candidate.md` compares the retained codec, header,
member, write, scheduler, memory, JSONL, whole-read, and concurrent-read rows
with exact `v2.0.0a4` for stdlib zlib and zlib-ng. All correctness-bearing
digests and sizes match. No accepted comparison is more than 10% slower;
every observation over 5% has a written disposition and retained raw samples.
Peak memory and scheduler gaps remain within the established gates.

The issue #86 10-byte diagnostic is 0.75% faster with forced stdlib and 0.02%
slower with zlib-ng in the controlled b1 comparison. Same-call write
visibility, failure attribution, cancellation, and poisoning semantics remain
unchanged. `plans/reviews/issue-86-b1-closeout.md` is ready for maintainer use;
no remote issue mutation was performed.
