# WP5 completion: G06, G07 and G08

This record maps each WP5 gate's plan requirements (§9.1–§9.3) to its evidence
and maintainer dispositions, for maintainer sign-off. The candidate for all
three gates is `2f78925`; `src/` has been frozen there since the WP5 timing
series began, so C0 can be 2f78925 or a docs-only descendant. G09/C0 and G17
remain separate and open.

## G06: generic long-line scaling

*Gate: generic long-line scaling measured; each confirmed issue repaired or
explicitly dispositioned.*

- **Measured** across `newline=None`, `""`, `"\n"`, `"\r"` and `"\r\n"`,
  UTF-8 and ISO-2022-JP, 1–16 MiB, chunk sizes 4 KiB, 64 KiB and 256 KiB,
  compressible,
  higher-entropy and incompressible content, terminators absent, at the end and
  split, and a final trailing CR. Append work, kept separate from scanning,
  and allocation are in the
  [resource comparison](../benchmarks/v2.0.0b2-wp5-longline-resources.md):
  peak memory is the tracemalloc Python peak, RSS is a before/after snapshot,
  and native peaks are not measured;
  quiet-machine timing and doubling ratios for both engines are in the
  [batch 2 results](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch2-results.md).
- **Repaired.** Generic lines accumulate as fragments plus one final join
  (`55c1e3e`, [record](v2.0.0b2-wp5-generic-lines.md)), preserving cookies,
  decoder replay, rollback and salvage, with focused tests in
  `tests/test_generic_line_fragments.py`. The unpublished-read cookie defect
  found on the way is corrected as BC6 (`cea195d`,
  [ledger](v2.0.0b2-behavior-exceptions.md)).
- **Effect.** The worst baseline cases improve by up to 84.83% (stdlib) and
  90.12% (zlib-ng). On generic paths the 8→16 MiB doubling medians fall from
  2.94–4.97 to 2.04–2.66.
- **Dispositions (maintainer, 2026-10-04):** the ISO-2022-JP 16 MiB `\n`/`\r`
  flags are a qualification-visible, order-dependent allocator-state effect,
  accepted without repair; candidate ratios above 2.5 show no
  candidate-specific regression; inconclusive rows are accepted without a speed
  claim.
- **Limits.** A returned line can be arbitrarily large; no constant-memory
  `readline()` is claimed, and no line-size limit was added. Existing
  decompressed-size limits stay enforced (the same 86 public cases as G07).
  Ratios are descriptive, with no universal complexity claim.

## G07: partial-read amplification

*Gate: partial-read read-ahead, allocation and latency measured; existing
resource limits remain enforced.*

- **Read-ahead and allocation measured** for `read(1)`, small `readinto()`,
  `peek(1)`, bounded `readline(size)` and `read1()`, binary and text,
  seekable and non-seekable, at 1, 16 and 32 MiB with one and four
  concurrent handles, separating per-chunk output from aggregate retention
  ([resource record](../benchmarks/v2.0.0b2-wp5-read-resources.md)).
  Allocation is the tracemalloc Python peak; RSS is a before/after snapshot,
  and native allocation peaks are not measured.
- **Latency measured.** Time to first result or error, and scheduling gaps,
  208 engine/case groups, six retained rounds across both engines
  ([batch 1 results](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch1-results.md#g07)). No group has zero
  ticks, no cohort max/min ratio exceeds 1.5, and expected error outcomes are
  preserved. Six of the planned seven rounds were accepted by the maintainer
  on 2026-10-03.
- **Existing limits enforced.** `max_decompressed_size` below, at and above the
  boundary, member boundaries inside one source item and a corrupt trailer
  after early reads: 86 public cases in `tests/test_wp5_partial_read_limits.py`.
  Limit failures deliver no forbidden bytes and are never validated EOF.
- **Disposition.** Eager drain is retained for b2; a demand-bounded reader
  needs a separate compatibility decision. No partially drained codec operation
  is retained across public calls.
- **User guidance.** [Untrusted input](../../docs/recipes.md#processing-untrusted-gzip-input)
  explains that small reads bound delivery, not retained plaintext or
  validation work, and recommends a decompressed-size ceiling and a bounded
  number of concurrent handles. No vulnerability claim is made.

## G08: cross-item fairness

*Gate: both streaming wrappers yield cooperatively across empty, tiny and ready
sources without read-ahead.*

- **Repair.** Per-stream item, work and output budgets carried across items
  (`c122df5`), the lifecycle matrix (`e492b4c`) and the small-item compression
  correction (`2f78925`), all as BC5. Checkpoints yield no `b""`, prefetch no
  item and create no producer task. Tests in `tests/test_stream_fairness.py`
  cover empty, tiny, empty-member, single large item and slow-destination
  sources, cancellation, early exit, source failure and both engines, with
  sibling progress captured before exhaustion and a subprocess watchdog for
  infinite sources ([progress record](v2.0.0b2-wp5-progress.md)).
- **Selected private budgets.** A stream checkpoints after 256 source items or
  16 MiB of source bytes, 1 MiB or 4,096 chunks of inline output, or 128 KiB
  or 8 steps without output. Empty items count before being skipped. New feed
  operations and executor waits do not reset the stream-wide counters; the
  whole budget resets at a deliberate checkpoint, while the consecutive
  no-output counters also reset on visible output. There is no public tuning
  API. The tradeoff is measured: sibling progress before exhaustion, item gaps
  at most 256 (8 for empty members), and the throughput costs accepted below.
  Cooperative work between checkpoints is bounded; no wall-clock preemption is
  promised.
- **Scheduling, both engines** ([batch 1 results](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch1-results.md#g08-scheduling)):
  the WP4 baseline gives zero sibling ticks in the empty, tiny and empty-member
  cases; 2f78925 lets the sibling progress, with item gaps bounded at 256 (8
  for empty members). No preemption bound is claimed.
- **Throughput.** stdlib against WP4 and WP5
  ([September 29](v2.0.0b2-wp5-nightly-20260929-results.md),
  [September 30](v2.0.0b2-wp5-nightly-20260930-results.md),
  [follow-up record](v2.0.0b2-wp5-followup-20260930.md)) and zlib-ng against
  both ([batch 1 results](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch1-results.md)).
- **Dispositions (maintainer, 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03,
  [G08 disposition](v2.0.0b2-wp5-g08-disposition.md)):** the 64-byte
  compression gains are claimed; the stdlib decompression residual against WP4
  (+1.40% to +2.92%) and the qualified zlib-ng small-item costs (at most
  +2.19%) are accepted; zlib-ng text-decompression speedups are not claimed,
  being a negative control for measurement noise; inconclusive rows on both
  engines are accepted without a speed claim; stdlib scheduling's three
  retained blocks are sufficient.

## Measurement process

The timing protocol, its quiet-policy changes (`ebcc67d`, `ee53619`) and the
nightly batch driver (`558687b`) are recorded in the
[protocol](v2.0.0b2-wp5-timing-protocol.md) and the
[batch 1](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch1.md) and [batch 2](v2.0.0b2-wp5-batch2.md)
records. Series run under different policies are not pooled. Every started
series is retained; rejected and deferred attempts are archived. Codex reviewed
every frozen plan before registration and every result before disposition.
Claims carry no statistical significance, zero-overhead, preemption-bound or
G17 qualification.

## Maintainer sign-off

The maintainer signed off on 2026-10-04 after Codex's requirement-by-requirement
review found no remaining gate-coverage findings. G06, G07 and G08 are closed.
The plan checklist, findings F5–F7, the qualification record and the C0 notes
are updated to match. G09/C0 and G17 remain open.
