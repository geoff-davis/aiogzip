Rows with at least three usable, direction-consistent blocks are qualified:
against WP4, random compress 64 and 1 MiB, random decompress 64, text compress
64 and 64 KiB, and text decompress 64, 64 KiB and 1 MiB; against WP5, random
compress 64, 4 KiB and 1 MiB, random decompress 64, text compress 64 and text
decompress 64. Every other row is formally inconclusive.

**Text decompression is a negative control, not a speedup.** f73ae8d to 2f78925
changes compression only, yet the WP5 comparison shows -3.9% to -4.7% text
decompression medians with high drift. That is measurement or order noise of
the same scale, and it confounds the qualified -6% WP4 text-decompression rows.
No speedup is claimed, and no production regression investigation is
indicated.

### Maintainer dispositions (2026-10-03)

1. **Small-item compression gains are claimed:** 64-byte compression against
   WP4 (random -16.53%, text -24.73%) and against WP5 (random -20.10%, text
   -29.33%), each with four usable blocks.
2. **Qualified small costs are accepted:** 64-byte decompression against WP4
   (random +1.73%, text +2.19%) and against WP5 (+0.62%, +0.90%), and random
   1 MiB compression (+0.74%, +0.75%).
3. **Text-decompression speedups are not claimed** and are recorded as the
   negative control above.
4. **Inconclusive rows are accepted without a speed claim,** including random
   decompression at 64 KiB and 1 MiB against WP4 (+3.95%, +4.74%, one usable
   block each) and the large text-compression gains. The maintainer accepts
   their uncertainty for G08; this neither establishes the absence of a
   regression nor upgrades the measurements. The zlib-ng throughput follow-up
   allowance is not used.

## G08 scheduling

Both engines show the intended qualitative repair. In the WP4 baseline, the
empty, tiny and empty-member cases give zero sibling ticks; with 2f78925 the
sibling makes progress before completion, with item gaps bounded at 256 (8 for
empty members). zlib-ng has four complete blocks, 56 samples per group. stdlib
lost block 3 to interference and keeps three blocks, 42 samples per group. This
is descriptive evidence, with no preemption bound.

**Maintainer disposition (2026-10-03):** the three-block stdlib series is
accepted as sufficient descriptive evidence.
