# b2 behavioral-exception ledger

WP0 records reproduced defects and the revision's authorization for bounded
repairs. WP1 implements BC1, WP2 implements BC2 and WP3 implements BC3 below;
WP10 implements BC7, BC8 and BC9, and accepts BC10 without a production change.
The 2.0.0rc1 plan adds BC11 onward.
Fourteen exact named source-failure corrections are allowlisted under BC2;
26 exact opening corrections are allowlisted under BC3. The original 25
compatibility scenarios remain unchanged. C0 does not exist.

Original reproductions use baseline `dc8950cb334e1cf4082f2bf50074464e06c72287`, Python 3.14.2,
aiofiles 25.1.0 and verified stdlib/zlib-ng modes. Exact tests and raw observations
are linked from [findings](v2.0.0b2-findings.md) and
[measurements](../benchmarks/v2.0.0b2-results.md). The public signature/type/codec
lifecycle manifest remains the b1 contract.

## BC1 selected correction

Only cancellation of an in-flight native codec advancement changes: cleanup waits
for its actual completion even under direct cancellation of the executor-owning task, its shield waiter,
or runner shutdown. Cancellation remains the caller-visible outcome; simultaneous
native exceptions are explicitly chained for diagnosis. Normal output, signatures,
exception classes, thresholds, executor selection and abandonment rules stay as
before. A blocked native call can delay cancellation without a fixed bound.
The original F1 tests become passing regressions; other finding markers remain.
Repair authorization is revision §1.2/WP1 and the instruction to proceed with WP1.
The final repair is `f39fb8e`, following `c27b48d`. Clean paired captures and
G02 closure are in the [WP1 completion record](v2.0.0b2-wp1-completion.md).
No compatibility-trace scenario needed an allowance.

## BC2 selected correction

The maintainer selected: “Make unknown-effect errors terminal; preserve retries
only with a provable no-effect guarantee.” This authorizes changing custom-source
exception retries as well as cancellation, resolving the separate decision required
by WP2 §6.2. Native aiofiles results are retained after settlement; custom failures
with unknown/changed cursor become terminal non-validation failures. An optional
authoritative synchronous byte cursor preserves genuine no-effect retries.

[Source ownership design](../design/v2.0.0b2-source-consumption.md) defines native
completion, one-result retention, cache invalidation, custom-source requirements,
and safe rewind/context exit. Public signatures, validation salvage, ownership
flags and original first-error classes remain unchanged. The initial repair is
`ddc8e95`; final native worker publication and atomic entry prevention are
`f66c92b`. The shared portable trace/timing harness is `a923f22`.

The [exact BC2 allowance](../../tests/data/source_failure_bc2.json) records full
before/after semantics for twelve custom-source cases and two native cases.
Four proven no-effect custom cases remain unchanged. Each engine is checked
against a clean b1 capture; no broad error or scenario exclusion is used.
[G03 completion and measured consequences](v2.0.0b2-wp2-completion.md) retain
correctness evidence separately from unresolved G17 performance qualification.

### BC2 post-G19 repair: abort cache hole

The G19 cross review (finding F1, reproduced by the implementing session and
by Codex) found that the WP2 repair `ddc8e95` could itself produce
suffix-only success, the outcome BC2 forbids. The case: a non-seekable
native source, an exceptional context exit while a native read is in flight,
and a failing close of the underlying file, which leaves the reader open and
broken. Exit settles the read, and the source has consumed the chunk, but
the reader dropped it without caching it. Two places did this:

- `_read_compressed_chunk` raised `read aborted…` before adding the
  returned chunk to the rewind cache;
- a cancelled native read kept its completed result as pending input only
  while read health was HEALTHY, which it no longer is after an abort.

A recovery `seek(0)` then replayed the cache across the gap. With six equal
gzip members, chunk size equal to the member length, and the read of member 1
in flight, `seek(0)` and `read()` returned members 0, 2, 3, 4 and 5 with no
error, on both engines and at C0. b1 returns all six.

At maintainer direction (2026-10-07) the repair retains known consumed input,
as b1 does, rather than invalidating the cache:

- the chunk is cached before the abort check;
- a cancelled native read keeps its completed result whenever the handle is
  still open, whatever its read health (closing clears it);
- the existing `max_rewind_cache_size` cap still disables replay when the
  chunk does not fit, so recovery is refused rather than resumed past it.

The healthy read path does the same work in a different order, so no timing
window was run, at maintainer direction. The error types and messages are
unchanged: the in-flight read still raises `read aborted…`. Regressions are
in `tests/test_abort_rewind_cache.py`: binary and text, settled and
cancelled reads, and the cache-cap refusal. Undoing either change fails the
tests for its path. The stateful generator never produces a failing abort
close, so the WP10 differential does not reach this case, and no predicate
is added.

## BC3 selected correction

WP3 reserves acquisition/initialization before suspension and rejects overlapping
open/close calls with `ConcurrentOperationError`. Acquired resources are published
only after initialization; cancellation waits for native acquisition/initialization
and closes unpublished owned resources. Failed opening preserves caller-owned
external streams for retry.

The initial reviewed repair is `3ef0266`. Follow-up qualification preserves existing
exception causes: a failed cleanup adds a note to the original exception rather than
overwriting its cause. Exceptional context exit during opening preserves the body's
error in both wrappers while the concurrent opener retains ownership; clean exit
rejects the conflict. The [ownership design](../design/v2.0.0b2-open-ownership.md)
records this policy. Final production is `1ee90a3`, shared harness `befb4d7`.
The [26 exact BC3 allowances](../../tests/data/open_bc3.json) and
[G04 completion](v2.0.0b2-wp3-completion.md) retain clean source/harness provenance,
full before/after values and local verification.

The [cleanup-cancellation review correction](v2.0.0b2-wp3-completion.md#cleanup-cancellation-review-correction)
in `003ca5f` restores b1 control flow when cancellation/interrupts arrive during
cleanup after an ordinary opening failure. Its eight added trace scenarios match
b1 exactly, so no BC3 allowance is added. Ordinary cleanup failures remain notes;
control-flow exceptions propagate after settlement, with original failure context
preserved. G04 was reopened and requalified locally.

## BC6 selected correction

Fast long-line reads and sized reads retain decoded fragments locally until they
publish them. With no buffered prefix, b1's `tell()` during such a read reported
the advanced decoder frontier, so the cookie skipped characters not yet
published, and replaying from it lost them. `cea195d` records the fragment
origin for that empty-prefix case and makes `tell()` use it. Publication clears
the origin, and reservation exit clears it on every exceptional path. Cookies
taken with a buffered prefix keep their b1 behavior.

The regression matrix covers readline, anext, sized read, `readlines(hint)` and
`iter_batches`, all five newline modes, UTF-8, UTF-16 and ISO-2022-JP, at
initial and midstream positions: 150 parametrized replay cases, plus four
failure and origin-cleanup cases. The module also holds two separate G06
xfails. Claude ran this focused module from `cea195d` against clean `v2.0.0b1`
source (import path verified) on 2026-09-29: 102 failed, 52 passed and 2
xfailed, with every failure at the post-`seek(cookie)` replay assertion.
Codex checked the test structure but did not rerun it.
Public signatures, exception classes and the b1 compatibility traces are
unchanged. C0 and the WP6 refactor must preserve this correction.
