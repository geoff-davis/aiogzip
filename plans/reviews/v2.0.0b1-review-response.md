## Corrective benchmark method

The first follow-up used three balanced process rounds per source tree and
engine, 21 timing samples per row, five allocation samples per row, CPU-0
affinity, and unchanged fixtures. Per-benchmark minima still moved together on
several byte-identical direct-codec rows, revealing process/source-tree bias.

The targeted follow-up therefore loaded exact a4 and b1 under distinct package
aliases in one process. After one warm-up per side, it ran 50 A/B/B/A cycles,
giving 100 samples per source tree and row with GC disabled. Correctness was
verified on every sample. All investigated minimum-to-minimum deltas were
below 5%:

- stdlib: -0.57% to +1.11%;
- zlib-ng: -0.68% to +4.68%;
- 256 KiB output-bound row: -0.26% stdlib and +0.09% zlib-ng.

The diagnostic is `benchmarks/investigate_b1_timing.py`; raw inputs and output
paths are listed in `plans/benchmarks/v2.0.0b1-candidate.md`.
